Electric Shift Warning Light
N5160103P0001000ENG
設計/目的
The electric shift warning light illuminates when a malfunction occurs in the electric shift control system.
DF100C5PAA00ENG
燈泡檢查
For 2 seconds after the ignition switch is turned 開啟.
與主警告燈同步
不適用
於儀表 CAN 通訊中斷或異常訊號時作動
有關儀表 CAN 通訊中斷的處理措施，請參閱失效保護.
系統圖
CF100C5QAA00ENG
訊號路徑
  • The electric shift control module transmits an electric shift malfunction 訊號 to the TCM when detecting malfunction in the electric shift system.
  • The TCM detects 故障碼 when TCM receives an electric shift malfunction 訊號.
  • The TCM transmits an electric shift malfunction 訊號, a shift 位置 訊號, and a neutral hold mode 訊號 to the 儀表 when detecting 故障碼 in TCM.
  • The ABS 作動器 and electric unit (control unit) transmits an 電動駐車煞車 status 訊號 to the 儀表.
  • The 儀表 turns 開啟 the electric shift warning light according to each 訊號. 請參閱燈光狀態
燈光狀態
當符合下列任一條件時。
  • Except neutral hold mode
  • Shift 位置 is except P 位置
  • Electric shift system malfunction (Transmission does not shift to P 位置 due to electric shift system malfunction)
  • 車輛 車速 is less than 5 km/h(3 MPH)
  • 駐車煞車 is not applied
熄滅條件
When any of the conditions listed below is satisfied:
  • In neutral hold mode
  • Shift 位置 is P 位置
  • Electric shift system is normal
  • 車輛 車速 is 5 km/h (3 MPH) or more
  • 駐車煞車已啟用
