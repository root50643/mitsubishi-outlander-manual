1.檢查 CAN Communication Line
檢查 CAN communication line.
  • 檢查 接頭 and terminals of related control units for 損壞, bending, or loose connections.
  • 檢查 線束 導通 between related control units. (Open/short circuit)
  • 檢查 that power supply circuit and ground circuit are normal at related control units.
  • 檢查 that resistance of termination circuits are normal.
  • 檢查 that operating status of related control units are normal.
