診斷條件
  • TCM 電源電壓：10 V 以上
  • CVT fluid temperature: -20°C (-4°F) or more
  • 引擎車速：More than 450 rpm
訊號
–
臨界值
When any of the following condition A or B is satisfied.
A) When all of the following conditions are satisfied.
  1. Secondary pressure sensed by the 感知器 is less than "Line pressure + Tolerance"
  2. Secondary pressure sensed by the 感知器 is less than "Target secondary pressure - Tolerance"
[Interval condition]
當符合下列所有條件時。
  • Target pressure change: "|Current target secondary pressure – Target secondary pressure at condition (a) and (b) are met|" is 1 Mpa or more
  • 車輛 stop: 車輛車速 is 3 km/h (1.9 MPH) or less
B) When all of the following conditions are satisfied.
  • |Target Primary pressure – Target Primary pressure before 10ms|: 0 Mpa or more
  • Target Primary pressure: 0.86 Mpa or more
  • Target Primary pressure – Current Primary pressure: More than 1.2 Mpa
診斷延遲時間
5 seconds × 2 times in the same DC (with interval below)
可能原因
  • 線束或接頭
  • 次壓力電磁閥
  • 控制電磁閥
  • TCM
失效保護
  • Lock-up is prohibited when 車輛車速 is low.
  • 引擎 torque is restricted.
故障碼確認程序
1.作業前準備
若剛才執行過另一項「故障碼確認程序」，請將點火開關關閉，並等待至少 10 秒後，再執行下一項測試。
測試條件：
執行下列程序前，請確認電壓在 10 V 以上。
转至 2.
2.執行故障碼確認程序
  1. 啟動引擎。
  2. Drive the 車輛 and maintain the following conditions for 20 seconds or more.
    Shift 位置: "D" 位置
    CVT fluid temperature*: More than 25°C (77°F), and less than 120°C (248°F)
    *: 檢查 CVT fluid temperature with M.U.T.-III SE.
    車速：40 km/h（25 MPH）或更高
    注意：
    請注意行車車速。
  3. 停止車輛。
  4. Repeat step 2 to 3.
  5. 檢查故障碼。
